Ryoku Filigree is co-authored by aethctl and Sipper1236.

Composition inspiration and collaboration credit:
https://github.com/Sipper1236/inspo-fastfetch

The Ryostore-native edition preserves the locked v18 Ryoku composition while
removing the standalone installer's machine-specific shim state. The renderer
is product-local, keeps its own fixed module list, takes its colours from
Ryoku's selected palette (~/.cache/ryoku/colors.json), and resolves the real
Fastfetch binary from the platform at runtime. It never reads
~/.config/fastfetch/config.jsonc: the composition is closed, so a Settings edit
cannot change the line count.

No code or artwork from Sipper1236/inspo-fastfetch is redistributed here unless
separate redistribution permission is provided. The shipped ornamental artwork
and Ryoku mark treatment are the Ryoku submission assets.

The renderer uses only local files and local system information. It contacts no
network services.

The OS key shows Tux rather than the NixOS mark, so the readout fits whichever
distribution Ryoku runs on.
