Dynamic Island, Ryoku bar style

Upstream: quickshell-DynamicIsland, https://github.com/Alan-Betty/quickshell-DynamicIsland
Version: 4.0.0
Licence: MIT, see LICENSE (Alan Betty).

Built from upstream's ryostore/barstyles/dynamic-island folder. In upstream:

- shared/ holds the island's QML and shader, the same code the standalone
  Hyprland build runs. tools/sync.sh copies it here verbatim.
- Platform.qml is written for Ryoku: notifications wrap shell.services Notifs,
  compositor state comes from the Wm facade, and Night Shift, Caffeine,
  session actions, screenshots and recording go through the shell's
  Nightlight, Flags, SessionActions, Capture and Recorder services. Launching
  goes through AppLaunch and Spawn.
- Scene.qml is the bar style entry. ShellShortcut.qml bridges global
  shortcuts through the Wm provider's GlobalShortcutBridge.

4.0.0

- Tray menus open inside the island; urgent banners expire; banner holds
  can't stick.
- Idle work trimmed: minute clock ticks, visualizer and position ticks only
  while visible, Pipewire mic detection, stats and network polls tied to
  their tab.
- One shared codebase with the standalone build, behind Platform.qml.
