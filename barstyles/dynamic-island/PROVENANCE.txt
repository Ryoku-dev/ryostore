Dynamic Island Ryoku Bar Style

Upstream: quickshell-DynamicIsland, https://github.com/Alan-Betty/quickshell-DynamicIsland
Landed from RyoStore issue #207 ("[Submit]: Dynamic Island Rich with Features").
Upstream license: MIT; see LICENSE (Alan Betty).

All upstream QML is carried (36 files: shell surfaces, island, banner stack,
notification center and cards, launcher, settings window, widgets) with the
following Ryoku rewrites:

- shell.qml is replaced by Scene.qml, the Ryoku bar product entry: one island
  overlay per screen, singletons armed on the primary, and the desktop's
  surface bus routes `launcher` and `settings` to the island's surfaces.
- IslandWindow.qml: Hyprland monitor/fullscreen state moves to the neutral Wm
  facade (Wm.focusedOutput, Wm.outputHasFullscreen). The hyprctl gaps_out
  probe becomes an "Outer gaps" setting. HyprlandFocusGrab is replaced by a
  neutral full-window input mask plus a click-away underlay.
- Island.qml: focusedMonitor lookup replaced by Wm.focusedOutput.
- Hub.qml: focusedScreen uses Wm; workspace ids/dots come from Wm.workspaces;
  session actions delegate to shell.services SessionActions; screen
  recording delegates to shell.services Recorder; screenshots delegate to
  shell.services Capture; Night Shift delegates to shell.services Nightlight
  (the hyprsunset process and the hyprctl session fallback are gone).
- Notifs.qml: rewritten as a wrapper over the shell's notification server
  (shell.services Notifs). The upstream daemon takeover (pkill of mako,
  dunst, swaync, fnott plus the bus-owner watcher) is removed; arrivals,
  grouping, banners, expiry and the Notification Center bookkeeping stay.
- Theme.qml: the hyprctl border accent source is replaced by a "ryoku"
  source reading shell.services Theme.primary; the hyprpaper wallpaper probe
  is replaced by a ryogami probe. Matugen, pywal, GTK and wallpaper sources
  stay as upstream.
- Config.qml: settings move to ~/.config/ryoku/dynamic-island/settings.json;
  reset applies mirrored defaults in place instead of reloading the shell.
  notification takeover and island-notifications-by-default sound knobs are
  re-defaulted as documented in the README.
- SettingsWindow.qml: daemon status/overwrite rows replaced by the shared
  daemon wording, the Hyprland binds page becomes the global-shortcut list,
  "Reload Island" (a whole-shell reload) is dropped.

Honest degradations are listed in README.md. Everything else — the glass
surface, springs, tabs, media, timer, launcher, weather and Bluetooth
pages — is carried unchanged from the pinned upstream main branch.
