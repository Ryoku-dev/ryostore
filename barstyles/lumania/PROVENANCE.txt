Lumania draws a thin frame around the screen and hangs seven notches on it: the
island at the top centre, the power menu at the top left, the tray at the top
right, the workspace rail down the left edge, the control centre in the bottom
right corner, the wallpaper filmstrip at the bottom centre, and the search lip
in the bottom left corner.

The port keeps that picture and replaces the Omarchy plumbing with Ryoku's:
workspaces and fullscreen state come from the Wm facade, system state and
actions from shell.services, Bluetooth from Quickshell.Bluetooth, media from
the Media service, notifications from the one resident Notifs server (through
services/NotificationModel.qml), the audio spectrum from AudioBars, and
wallpapers from ryogami. The wallpaper colour engine, the keyboard backlight
sync, the voice-call view and the timer engine were left out.

The search lip no longer blooms into a search panel: it opens Ryoku's app
launcher instead, which ships beside this style as the Lumania launcher. Its
right-click opens this style's own settings panel.

Settings are written to ~/.config/ryoku/barstyle-lumania.json.
