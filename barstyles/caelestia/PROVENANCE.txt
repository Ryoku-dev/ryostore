Caelestia Ryoku Bar Style

Source: Caelestia shell, https://github.com/caelestia-dots/shell (GPL-3.0)
Via: the KDE port by ladybug-me, https://github.com/ladybug-me/caelestia-kde, commit 6769205

Maintained by: dodo986 (from 2026-10-24)

The Caelestia shell's own bar, dashboard, session menu, sidebar, OSD and popouts, running
inside the Ryoku shell. Upstream ships a C++ plugin; this item replaces it with plain QML:
the config and design tokens are generated from the plugin's defaults, and system data
comes from Ryoku's shell services.
