KITSUNE — provenance

The face is 0xSrcAura's own work, built 2026-10-21 for Jurai shell:
Ase, the seven-tailed kitsune, and her seven tarot folds — his lock
and login composition, first raised on his own machines, preview-
proven the night it was built (typing binds the folds one by one;
at seven the Cast rises; a refusal hurts her and scatters the
cards; the true key transforms her and opens the seal).

Two roots were written then, and both are preserved in this repo:

  content/Main.qml   — the greeter root, seated over SDDM's own
                       machinery (and, through Ryoku's SddmShim,
                       over the session lock as well). Unmodified.
  faces/lock/Main.qml — the lock-face root, driven by the shell's
                       threshold `gate` contract. Kept as the
                       record of the original seat; not installed.

Her scene (content/gotchi/, the Insei* files and their qmldir),
her shaders (content/shaders/, .frag sources beside the compiled
.qsb), and her palette-and-accents singleton
(content/tw/TransparentWorld.qml) come from the Jurai shell tree
(apsara/mandara/soul/), with the small standalone-port changes
listed below.

The port's own hand, kept as small as the Fūin port's:

  * content/tw/ArOrchestrator.qml — a resting singleton under the
    real one's name. In the shell he is the AR stage's living
    state; the one scene seat that reads him (InseiHub) is never
    raised at the door, so his shape stands here at rest and the
    scene files stay untouched.
  * content/tw/qmldir — declares the two singletons; a greeter
    registers singletons only when their folder stands as a module.
  * content/theme.conf + metadata.desktop — the store/greeter
    dress: the one config key her greeter reads
    (AllowUppercaseLettersInUsernames=false, her original rule)
    and her name plate.
  * Her ground: the greeter still looks for a theme-local
    Backgrounds/wallpaper.jpg under a manager-published still at
    /var/lib/valence-greeter/wallpaper.jpg. Neither is bundled —
    where neither exists she stands on the veiled dark, exactly
    as the sealed resting state was drawn.
  * The ground's veil (v1.0.1, the captain's word the day she
    landed: the current wallpaper shows through the lock, blurred
    for privacy): the theme-local layer now wears the same
    MultiEffect blur and dimming as the manager layer. In the
    shell it stood raw beneath the blurred still; standing alone
    in a store theme it would have shown the picture plain at
    the door. On Ryoku her Backgrounds seat is a symlink to the
    current wallpaper, re-struck by tools/sync-local.sh after
    every install.
  * v1.0.2, the same day, also his art direction: the card fan
    rides 44 units lower in InseiScene (her arc centre), nested
    closer over Ase's head, and the hint line above the arc —
    "read the sigils · invoke your sequence", half-eaten by the
    centre card as built — is lifted clear so it reads whole.
    The v1.0.2 submission changed those two numbers.
  * The success whisper welcomes the account entered in the greeter
    instead of naming the theme's author for every user.
  * The sign-in column sits at the foot of the screen so the
    invocation whisper stays clear at the supported 720p size.
  * 2026-10-27 — the ground becomes the desktop itself: a third
    layer reads /run/user/<uid>/fuin-desktop.png (cache off), the
    screenshot tools/fuin-lock takes on the way into the lock,
    held to the same blur and dimming; the two wallpaper layers
    were lifted to match (blur 0.8, brightness −0.10, scrim
    0.30), so capture-less showings — the greeter, an idle lock —
    stand on the same veil. The capture lives only in the tmpfs.
  * 2026-10-27 — the pulse: content/shaders/pulse.frag, a filament
    field over the ground at a whisper, with a sporadic surge
    front and a rare crackle flare, driven from Main.qml. Baked
    with the qt6 shader set, like the rest of her shaders.
  * 2026-10-27 — the name plate at the foot of the face
    ("0XSRCAURA") is removed. The face carries no signature now.
  * 2026-10-27 — the mantra gate, restored to the design she was
    built for: theme.conf keys MantraGate / MantraOrder deal the
    folds shuffled at every showing; digit keys or card taps
    answer slot numbers in the reader's own order; a wrong
    sequence redeals; a right one opens the password field — or,
    when MantraPass is set on a private seat, submits the login
    itself. The switch at the bottom right lifts the gate for one
    showing. Shipped off; the public theme.conf carries no one's
    order and no key.
  * 2026-10-27 — the invoke beat and the answering glass: a spoken
    sequence no longer fires its own verdict — at seven the Cast
    rises in mantra mode too, and Enter (or the Cast) submits, so
    a mis-spoken digit can still be unspoken first. And the pulse
    shader learned touch: a click or tap lands in the ground as a
    bloom and an outward ring at that point, card taps included
    (the scene's cardTapped now carries the point).
  * 2026-10-27 — the touch learned water: the ripple is now a
    decelerating front with two trailing crests, a spectral
    split at each rim, and a bright core line — prismatic water,
    still threaded through the filament field.
  * 2026-10-27 — the water made true: the pulse now samples the
    ground (a ShaderEffectSource over the ground stack, the
    original still standing beneath as the fallback) and bends
    it along the wavefront — refraction, not just light. Crests
    went fine (a bright core at a third the old width), each
    ring carries a lit side and a shaded side, and a capillary
    shimmer rides inside the train.
  * 2026-10-27 — the gate's discipline, and a face that explains
    itself: wrong sequences now count — at the limit (MantraAttempts,
    3 standing) the gate stands down to the password field for the
    rest of the showing, and nothing stands it again but the manual
    switch. A refused stored key stands it down too; the old
    auto-rearm is gone. A settings button (bottom left) opens a
    panel: the gate switch, the attempt limit for the showing,
    whether a key is set, and a plain account of what the gate
    does and where its standing choices live.
  * 2026-10-27 — the panel is a hint now: the bottom-left word
    reads "hint", the panel is titled "MANTRA GATE — THE HINT",
    and the four corner words (hint, mantra, reboot, power off)
    bloom on hover — a soft shadow off the word itself, easing
    in and out. The mantra word is part of its switch now:
    hovering or clicking the word works the toggle.

## 2026-10-27 — 2.0.0: the companion

kitsune-mantra joins the package (content/companion/): a
session-side window reusing Ase's own scene, where the order is
spoken by tapping the cards or typing their numbers, with a
practice mode that deals shuffled fans like the door. Its
writer (mantraconf.py) is the only thing that writes
theme.conf — one JSON line in on stdin, an atomic write out at
mode 600, public state back, never the key. The shipped
theme.conf now documents MantraAttempts, and the hint panel
names the companion; its keys (h, g, −/+, esc) work the panel
from the keyboard. The gate's rules did not change.

2026-10-27 — 2.0.0 audit pass. The companion is hardened:
the writer refuses malformed input and leaves theme.conf
untouched; a seal that goes unanswered is reported; the
writer is found without environment help. The shell moved
to companion/run/: quickshell registers its config
directory as an import context, under which MantraSetup's
relative scene import does not resolve, and Qt.resolvedUrl
returns the blackhole scheme there — the shell now stands
alone and loads MantraSetup by absolute URL from
Quickshell.configDir. Launch: qs -p <theme>/companion/run.
Proven from a copy of the installed layout: the window
renders whole and reads the standing settings.
