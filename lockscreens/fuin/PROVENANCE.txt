Fūin 封印 — provenance
======================

The face is Jurai's Threshold, the lock screen of the Jurai shell
(github.com/SourceAura/jurai-shell), carried into the SDDM greeter
contract by its author. Threshold was written against a small "gate"
contract — the face never learns who checks the password — and already
served three hosts in the shell (the session lock on PAM, the greetd
greeter, and the gate apsara). This theme is its fourth host: Main.qml
implements the gate against SDDM (sddm.login, userModel, sessionModel),
and the face's files stand as they are in the shell.

Carried verbatim but for their imports (the shell's qs.* modules resolve
to the local singletons below): Threshold.qml, Dial.qml, MinuteHand.qml,
Portrait.qml, Rim.qml, Logotype.qml, Mark.qml.

Written for this seat:
- Main.qml — the SDDM gate adapter, the user/session choices at the
  foot (shown only when a real choice exists), and the welcome kept
  faithful (the session handover is SDDM's, and the greeter may exit
  while the welcome is still on screen — as with every welcoming theme).
- Theme.qml / Timing.qml / Metrics.qml / Typography.qml — the shell's
  token singletons with their derivations intact and their sources
  fixed: the palette is Jurai's shipped default ("Jurai": background
  #12160F, text #EAF1E6, primary #B9DE6B, secondary #3FBFA0; the alert
  state colour #FF6B5D), timings at speed 1.0, fonts by role.
- Config.qml — the bridge from Jurai's dotted keys to theme.conf.
- SystemClock.qml — a Timer-driven namesake for Quickshell's clock, so
  Threshold's bindings stand unchanged.
- Insei.qml — an empty seat, on purpose: the shell shows Insei's
  medallion only while she is present, and a greeter has no Insei to
  witness. The medallion code stands verbatim, hidden by her own
  absence rule.
- WallpaperImage.qml — the shell's component minus its span machinery
  (which reads Quickshell's screen list): a greeter shows one screen
  its own picture whole; fill and fit are unchanged.

One repair against upstream: Metrics gains `cellHeight` (30, the row
unit). Threshold's portrait badge has always sized itself by it; the
shell's Metrics never defined it, and the badge's glass silently
collapsed. Here it stands at its intended width.

No photograph is bundled. Jurai's own lock falls back to a forest
photograph inside the shell; a greeter theme shipping a stranger's
wallpaper would be the wrong default, so Fūin's resting state is the
drawn, veiled dark — and theme.conf's `background` seats any picture
of the machine's own.

Fonts (content/fonts): Spectral and Orbitron, SIL Open Font License
1.1 (see content/fonts/OFL.txt), from the Google Fonts library.
The mark in Logotype/Mark is Jurai's Tree-Thunder （樹雷）, drawn in QML.

Two seating notes from the port. The theme ships a qmldir declaring
its singletons and components: SDDM's greeter resolves sibling types
from the folder but registers `pragma Singleton` files only when the
folder stands as a module — without it the face loads and paints
nothing. And Threshold declares its gate `required`, so Main.qml's
Loader carries the gate as a property and the component binds to it
at creation; a gate assigned after creation never lands.
