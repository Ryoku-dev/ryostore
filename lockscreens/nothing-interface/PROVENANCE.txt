Nothing Interface qylock theme

Original QML and helper scripts by Ivan, 2026, under the MIT License in this
folder. The design is an independent fan interpretation of Nothing OS.

No Nothing wallpaper, proprietary font, logo or AI-generated wallpaper is
installed with this lockscreen. During an in-session lock, the theme reads the
current Ryogami wallpaper, so it changes together with the desktop wallpaper.
It shows the local battery state, Ryoku's daily screen-time counter, and MPRIS
media metadata. The media control uses playerctl. In the system greeter, live
session-only data is unavailable and the background falls back to charcoal.

The catalogue preview is a capture of the interface, not part of the installed
theme. The wallpaper and album art visible in that capture are not redistributed
as standalone assets.
