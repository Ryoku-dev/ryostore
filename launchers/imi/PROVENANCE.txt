Imi launcher for the Ryoku desktop.

Overview search derived from the ii desktop shell family (pedrovfx's
fork of end-4's dots-hyprland, GPL-3.0), sharing its UI files with the
imi barstyle. Runs as a store launcher: services run on the shell's own
service facade, app launches go through the host AppLaunch, and window
results come from the host Wm interface.
Maintainer: dodo98 (https://github.com/dodo98).
