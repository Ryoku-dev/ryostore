Mandara — provenance

Born in Jurai shell as the court's palace: an AI core in Python,
a lineage of ROMs, organs and a war room around an omnibar. Ruled
a launcher in 2026 and cut down to it — what crossed into this
repo is the omnibar and its manners, and nothing else:

  * The face: one centred dark-glass plate, the input's voice
    ("speak or command"), the beam, quiet rows that answer with
    a glyph, a name, and a hint of what the row is.
  * The rune chips and the locking segments: TAB locks a lane
    and the lock rides above the field as a crystal token in
    the Obelisk runes' shape (RuneChip.qml, re-dressed lean —
    the SDF crystallize shader stayed with the shell; it can
    return when the chips learn their deeper work).
  * Her name, lettered small at the foot.

Left behind, deliberately: the Python core (routers, bridge,
venv — the mind passes to Manteion's keeping, private), the
ROM lineage (sibyls-rom, ase, conduit-sage, shigurui), the
organs and the speculum, the daemon readouts. ROMs are not a
separate world here; anything that runs is an ordinary result.

Written fresh against Ryoku's launcher contract (AUTHORING.md):
DesktopEntries for apps, AppLaunch to start them, Tokens for
colour, type and motion — she follows the user's scheme, scale
and reduced-motion setting. No Ryoku Team assets.
