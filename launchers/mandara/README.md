# Mandara

The Jurai suite's launcher for Ryoku. Summon her, type, run.

One mouth, four lanes, in order:

- **APPS** — your desktop entries, fuzzy-matched. Enter runs.
- **COMMAND** — a leading `>`, or a query nothing else answers,
  runs it in the shell.
- **ACTIONS** — her own verbs: lock the screen, take a screenshot,
  sleep.
- **FILES** — a path-shaped query (`/`, `~/`) walks folders;
  plain words search your home. Enter opens; a folder descends.

**TAB locks a lane.** The lock crystallizes as a rune chip above
the field — a crystal token in the shape of the Obelisk's runes
from Jurai shell. Backspace on an empty field releases it, ESC
clears before it closes, and she never lingers after a launch.
What locked segments will one day compose is theirs to become;
in v1 the chip locks a lane, and a lane is enough.

She is the omnibar soul of the old Mandara palace with the court
stripped away — no AI core, no organs, no ceremony between the
keystroke and the thing running. One dark-glass plate, centred,
her mark 曼 ghosted behind it, the prism beam under the input,
her name lettered small at the foot.

Install (RyoStore): the `mandara` app launcher, then pick her in
Settings -> App Launcher. Super+Space summons her from then on.
