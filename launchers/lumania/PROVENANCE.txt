The panel is the search notch from Lumania's bottom-left corner: it blooms out
of the frame with the search line over a grid of apps, recents first. The
command runner and the timer command were left out; apps come from
DesktopEntries and start through AppLaunch. Recents are kept in
~/.local/state/ryoku/lumania-recents.json, and the hidden-apps list in Ryoku's
own ~/.config/ryoku/launcher.json is honoured.
